Support Options and Rates

Care-First In-Home Support

Starting at $40/hour

2-hour minimum

For support that is primarily care-centered, where deeper planning, research, documentation, or follow-up are limited.

Respite

Caregiver breathing room, appointments, work time, rest, or another capable adult helping hold part of the day.

After-School Care

Recurring support through the after-school part of the day, including care, decompression, transitions, and the rhythms that help everyone get through the evening.

Backup Care

Temporary support when the usual care plan falls through or your family needs another care option.

Occasional Care

Occasional care when you need support without an ongoing recurring schedule.

Seasonal Care

Care during school breaks, summer, holidays, or other seasons when your family’s usual schedule changes.

How Scope and Pricing Stay Clear

Some support will have more than one need. Recurring family support, research-heavy work, advocacy preparation with additional follow-up, or blended projects may need a custom written scope based on the actual work involved.

Before support begins, we agree on what the work includes and what it will cost. Depending on the support, that may include the focus, dates or hours, location, travel, child-related care, caregiver participation, communication, documentation, materials, and a review or end point.

Pricing is part of that conversation. We may sometimes use an intentionally reduced or individually arranged price when it is sustainable to do so. Those arrangements are private and do not change the value or standard pricing of the work.

Nothing quietly becomes unlimited messaging, research, documentation, or rolling access.

IFC is not therapy, clinical treatment, legal representation, special-education representation, formal case management, medical care, custody evaluation, school evaluation, or crisis support.
